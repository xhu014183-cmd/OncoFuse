# Research-use disclaimer

This repository is a software demonstration for multimodal evidence plumbing.
It is not a medical device, does not provide a diagnosis, and must not be used
to make treatment decisions.

The imaging measurements are derived from supplied segmentation masks. The
longitudinal lesion matching and progression signals are transparent research
heuristics. They are not RECIST, mRECIST, LI-RADS, or a substitute for review
by a qualified radiologist.

All bundled examples are synthetic. Do not upload identifiable patient data to
public deployments. Brand names used in architectural discussions do not imply
endorsement, certification, or an official commercial collaboration.

Optional third-party image encoders are research adapters. Their embeddings
are not validated HCC biomarkers and do not change the deterministic verdict.
Model-code licenses may differ from the rights attached to training data and
weights. In particular, the optional M3D-CLIP path requires a separate legal
review before any commercial use.
